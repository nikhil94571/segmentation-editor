Segmentation editor: style board

Five looks for the same editor, same layout, same image. Judge each on two things: how it feels, and whether the tissue and its outline stay the most visible thing on screen. Mock-ups only: nothing here is clickable.

What I'd pick

B, the glass style on a neutral dark ground, with A as its light theme. It keeps the rounded floating panels you like, and it fixes the one real problem with the lavender version: a coloured surround shifts how pink and purple stain looks, and people annotating tissue are judging exactly those colours. In both A and B the area around the image is plain grey for that reason.

If the lab's experts spend hours a day in it, D is the most practical: smallest chrome, most room for the image, mid-grey surround like other imaging tools. It is also the least distinctive.

Whichever you choose, three things stay fixed because they are functional: the area behind the image is neutral grey, the region outline keeps a white halo so it reads on any tissue, and nodes stay orange so they never blend with the blue shading.
